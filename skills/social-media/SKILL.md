# Social Media

Be consistently useful on the few platforms where the audience already
spends time, in the formats those platforms reward, and answer people like a
human. Nothing is published, scheduled, replied to or sent from the user's
accounts without their explicit confirmation.

## When to use

- "What should we post?", "which platforms should we be on?"
- Building a content calendar or a batch of post drafts.
- Writing a reply playbook or handling a complaint or pile-on.
- Reviewing what worked last month and why.

## When not to use

- Long-form content strategy and briefs: `content-marketing`.
- Paid social campaigns and budgets: `paid-advertising`.
- Running a Discord, forum or member community: `community-management`.
- YouTube channel strategy and video editing: `youtube-channel`.
- Brand voice and positioning are undefined: `brand-discovery` first.

## Workflow

1. **Audit and set goals.** List current accounts, follower counts, posting frequency and the ten best and worst posts of the last 90 days. Set one goal per platform (awareness, traffic, community, hiring, support) and the metric that shows it.

   **Check:** A one-page audit names each platform's goal and metric.

2. **Choose platforms.** Start with two or three where the audience is active (table below). Claim the handle elsewhere, but do not spread effort thin.

   **Check:** Each chosen platform has a stated audience and a sustainable cadence.

3. **Define content pillars.** Three to five recurring themes that sit where audience problems and brand expertise overlap, for example: how-to, behind the scenes, customer stories, industry commentary, product news. Keep product promotion a minority of posts.

   **Check:** Every planned post maps to a pillar.

4. **Build the calendar** in a Google Sheet with columns: date, platform, pillar, format, hook, copy, asset link, alt text, link with UTM, owner, status (draft, approved, scheduled, published). Plan two to four weeks ahead and leave room for reactive posts.

   **Check:** The cadence is one the team can sustain for three months.

5. **Create native content.** Put the hook in the first line or first two seconds; caption every video; add alt text to images; use one call to action. Upload video natively rather than linking out when reach matters. Label realistic AI-generated or altered media where platform policy requires it.

   **Check:** Each draft follows the platform's current format guidance; verify character and length limits in its help centre rather than from memory.

6. **Approve, then publish.** Present drafts as an artifact for review. Publish, schedule, reply, DM, follow or like only after explicit user confirmation for each batch.

   **Check:** The calendar status shows "approved" with the approver's name before anything is scheduled.

7. **Reply using a playbook** (below). Set a response-time target per platform. Escalate anything involving safety, legal threats, personal data or a possible incident.

   **Check:** Every escalation category has a named owner.

8. **Measure monthly.** Per post and per pillar: reach, engagement rate (engagements divided by reach, defined once and used consistently), saves and shares, link clicks via UTM, and conversions. Treat follower growth as secondary. Keep what works, cut what does not.

   **Check:** The monthly review names at least one change to the calendar.

## Platform fit

| Platform | Typical strength | Watch out for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| LinkedIn | B2B, hiring, founder and employee voices | Link posts often get less reach than native text and documents |
| YouTube | Search-driven tutorials, long-form, Shorts | High production effort; see `youtube-channel` |
| Instagram, TikTok | Short vertical video, consumer and creator audiences | Trends move fast; needs frequent video |
| X, Bluesky, Threads, Mastodon | Real-time conversation, tech and developer audiences | Fragmented audiences; tone matters |
| Reddit | Niche communities and honest feedback | Strict self-promotion rules per subreddit; disclose affiliation |

## Reply playbook

| Comment type | Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| Question | Answer directly or link to the exact doc; follow up if unresolved |
| Praise | Thank specifically; ask to share or reshare with permission |
| Complaint | Acknowledge, apologize for the experience, move to a private channel, follow up publicly when resolved |
| Bug or outage | Confirm awareness, link the status page, avoid promising dates |
| Abuse, harassment or spam | Do not engage; hide, block or report per platform rules |
| Legal threat, safety issue or personal data | Do not reply; escalate to the named owner immediately |

## Disclosure and crisis

- Disclose material connections: paid partnerships, free products, affiliate links and employees posting about their employer. The US FTC Endorsement Guides and UK advertising rules require clear disclosure; use platform branded-content tools and plain labels such as "#ad".
- In a crisis, pause scheduled posts, publish one approved holding statement, keep a log, and only remove comments that break platform rules or your published policy.

## Anti-patterns

| Don't | Do instead | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Be on every platform | Two or three done well | Thin effort everywhere builds nothing |
| Cross-post identical copy | Adapt format and tone per platform | Each platform rewards its native formats |
| Mostly product announcements | Mostly useful content, mapped to pillars | Audiences unfollow pure promotion |
| Argue in public | Acknowledge, take it private, follow up | Public arguments escalate and get screenshotted |
| Buy followers or engagement | Earn them | Platforms penalize it and it corrupts your metrics |
| Undisclosed sponsorships | Clear labels and branded-content tools | Legal requirement and trust |
| Auto-post without review | Explicit approval per batch | Posts are public and screenshots outlive deletion |

## Verification checklist

- [ ] Each platform has a goal, a metric and a sustainable cadence.
- [ ] Every post maps to a pillar and has alt text and UTM-tagged links.
- [ ] Platform limits and policies were checked against current help pages.
- [ ] Sponsored or affiliated content is disclosed.
- [ ] The reply playbook names escalation owners.
- [ ] Nothing was posted, scheduled, replied to or sent without explicit user confirmation.
