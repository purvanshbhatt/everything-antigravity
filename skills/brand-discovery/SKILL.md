# Brand Discovery & Positioning

## Overview
This skill guides the agent in establishing a sharp, differentiated brand identity for any product, developer tool, open-source project, or startup.

## Core Framework
1. **Purpose & Mission**: Why does this project exist beyond commercial gains? What friction or pain in the world does it eradicate?
2. **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)**:
   - Industry, company size, developer seniority, tech stack.
   - Primary pain points, daily blockers, and emotional triggers.
3. **Competitive Moat & Positioning**:
   - Direct competitors vs indirect alternatives.
   - Distinctive positioning statement: *"For [Target Audience] who [State of Need], [Product Name] is a [Product Category] that [Key Benefit]. Unlike [Alternative], our product [Primary Differentiator]."*
4. **Tone & Personality**:
   - Archetype (e.g., The Engineer's Engineer, The Visionary Pioneer, The Trusted Guardian).
   - Voice parameters: technical depth, clarity, humor index, and jargon tolerance.

## Output Deliverables
- Executive Brand Brief (1-page markdown).
- Tone & Style Guide with example do's and don'ts.
- Elevator pitch variations (1-sentence, 30-second, and 2-minute).
