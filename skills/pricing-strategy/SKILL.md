# SaaS & Open-Core Pricing Strategy

## Overview
Design, test, and optimize pricing tiers, unit economics, packaging, and monetization models for software products and AI services.

## Pricing Archetypes
1. **Open-Core Model**:
   - Free OSS: Core features, community support, local execution, standard plugins.
   - Pro / Team: Hosted sync, team collaboration, centralized telemetry, enterprise audit logs.
   - Enterprise: Dedicated VPC, custom SLAs, SSO/SAML, compliance certifications (SOC2, HIPAA).
2. **Usage & Token-Based Billing**:
   - Cost-plus pricing on token usage (e.g. Gemini 1.5 Pro / Flash pass-through with margin).
   - Credits model: Prepaid balance with automatic top-ups and spending alerts.
3. **Feature Tiering Matrix**:
   - Value metric alignment: Tie price directly to where value is derived (e.g., active agents, processed hours, team seats).

## Deliverables
- Tier comparison matrix (Free vs Pro vs Enterprise).
- Pricing page FAQ addressing billing, seat adjustments, and refund policies.
- Unit economic sensitivity analysis.
