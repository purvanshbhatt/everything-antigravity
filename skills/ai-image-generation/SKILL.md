# Generative AI Image Synthesis & Prompt Engineering

## Overview
Formulate high-precision visual prompts, compositional constraints, lighting setups, and stylistic parameters for modern AI image generators.

## Prompt Architecture Formula
`[Subject & Action] + [Environment & Setting] + [Composition & Framing] + [Lighting & Atmosphere] + [Artistic Style & Medium] + [Technical Parameters]`

### Example
*"A futuristic developer workstation with glowing holographic Antigravity agent nodes floating above a sleek glass desk, cinematic wide shot, volumetric cyan and magenta rim lighting, cyberpunk aesthetic, shot on 35mm anamorphic lens, 8k resolution, photorealistic."*

## Composition & Lighting Guide
- **Framing**: Wide shot, medium close-up, macro detail, bird's-eye view, isometric perspective.
- **Lighting**: Volumetric lighting, golden hour, diffuse softbox, dramatic chiaroscuro, studio rim lighting.
- **Aspect Ratios**:
  - `16:9`: YouTube thumbnails, desktop hero banners, landscape previews.
  - `1:1`: Avatars, product square mockups, social feeds.
  - `9:16`: Mobile wallpapers, TikTok/Shorts/Reels backgrounds.
