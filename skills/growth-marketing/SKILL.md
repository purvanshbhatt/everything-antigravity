# Growth Marketing & Distribution

## Overview
Guides the creation of high-velocity organic distribution strategies, viral growth loops, and developer-first adoption engines.

## Tactical Playbooks
1. **GitHub Trending & Open-Source Virality**:
   - Compelling hero graphics and animated GIFs demonstrating instant utility in the first 5 seconds.
   - One-line quickstart command (`curl -sSL ... | bash` or `npx ...`).
   - Clear contribution guidelines, good first issues, and public roadmap transparency.
2. **Launch Acceleration (Product Hunt / Hacker News / X)**:
   - Hacker News "Show HN": Technical story, no marketing fluff, open source code, architectural lessons learned.
   - Product Hunt: High-contrast gallery media, animated makers comment, interactive demo links.
   - X / Twitter: High-density visual threads highlighting the exact problem, the failure of existing solutions, and the demo clip.
3. **Product-Led Growth (PLG) Loops**:
   - Time-to-value (TTV) reduction: user achieves core value in under 3 minutes.
   - Viral mechanics: shareable artifacts, team invites, badges, and exportable reports.

## Metrics & Tracking
- Activation Rate (Signups / Installs -> First core action).
- K-Factor (Viral coefficient).
- 30-Day Cohort Retention.
